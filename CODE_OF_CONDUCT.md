# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for all contributors, mentors, and participants.

## Expected Behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Welcome constructive feedback and differing viewpoints.
- Communicate clearly and courteously.
- Focus on collaboration and shared learning.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive language.
- Personal attacks, intimidation, or insulting comments.
- Disruptive or abusive behavior in issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or refuse contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to all project-related communication and participation, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and discussion channels.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness inappropriate behavior, report it to the project maintainers or the internship mentor through the appropriate official communication channel.
